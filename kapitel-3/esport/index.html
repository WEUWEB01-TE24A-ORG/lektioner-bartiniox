Latest Updates from the eSports World

Natus Vincere (NAVI) defeated Team Falcons 2–1 during the IEM Beijing 2026 tournament. The match was very exciting, and NAVI showed great form. The tournament takes place in China, with 16 teams competing.

The FC Pro Open Global Qualifier will take place in London on November 6–7. A total of 64 players from around the world will compete in the tournament. The 16 best players will qualify for the next stage of the FC Pro Open. It is one of the most important competitions of the new EA FC season.

The Fortnite Performance Evaluation tournament begins on November 5. It is a special Battle Royale competition where players compete in teams of three. The event aims to test the tournament system and game performance at a high competitive level. The best teams can advance to the next round.

Riot Games has announced the partner teams that will compete in the VCT during the 2027 season. The announcement marks the beginning of preparations for the new season. Teams are now planning their rosters and looking for new players to compete for international titles.
